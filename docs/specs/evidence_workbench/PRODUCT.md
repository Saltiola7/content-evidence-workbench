# Content Evidence Workbench Product Intent

## Users

- content and SEO/AEO engineers inspecting evidence quality
- AI engineers evaluating retrieval and citation behavior
- consultants demonstrating a fixed-scope evidence audit
- recruiters evaluating agentic, search, and data-platform engineering

## Core Journeys

1. Open synthetic corpus and understand document quality and lineage.
2. Upload bounded supported documents and validate before indexing.
3. Compare lexical and latent-semantic retrieval for a query.
4. Inspect exact cited chunks and entity relationship context.
5. Accept, reject, annotate, and reorder evidence.
6. Evaluate retrieval when relevance judgments are supplied.
7. Export citations, review decisions, metrics, configuration, and audit data.

## Success Evidence

- useful deterministic workflow without API credentials
- exact citations survive every ranking and review operation
- results distinguish retrieved evidence from generated interpretation
- no hidden persistence or fabricated relevance truth
- keyboard-usable responsive browser demo
- source, Pages, and Molab commit identities aligned at release

## Non-goals

- arbitrary web crawling or OCR
- multi-user knowledge base
- persistent vector or graph database
- owner-funded model calls
- authoritative factual verification
- generated-answer replacement for source review
